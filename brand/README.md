# SmartBiz Logo

## Direction

The SmartBiz mark is a compact shelf-shaped **S** inside a rounded app tile. The two pale crossbars reference stock shelves and ledger rows, while the warm check signal represents a completed sale or action needing attention.

It is designed to fit the current product aesthetic:

- confident blue from the mobile app: `#1B5EFF`
- deep operational ink from the UI: `#22314D`
- soft blue highlight: `#CFE0FF`
- warm action/status signal: `#F97316`
- off-white mark color: `#F8FAFE`

## Files

- `smartbiz-mark.svg`: standalone app mark for icons, splash screens, sidebar tiles, and compact headers.
- `smartbiz-lockup.svg`: horizontal logo lockup for onboarding, auth, marketing, and documents.

## Usage

Use the standalone mark at small sizes. Use the lockup when there is enough room for the wordmark and supporting context.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of the tile width. Avoid placing the mark on saturated blue or dark ink backgrounds unless it sits inside its tile.
