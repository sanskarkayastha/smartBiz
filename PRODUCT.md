# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nepali small business owners: shopkeepers, grocery vendors, and small retailers who run physical shops. They use the app on Android phones throughout the workday to record sales, check inventory, and track customer credit. They are not tech-heavy users but are familiar with smartphones. The context is a busy counter environment: quick glances, one-handed use, under time pressure.

## Product Purpose

SmartBiz replaces pen-and-paper bookkeeping and mental math for Nepal's small shops. It tracks inventory, records sales at the POS, manages customer due amounts, and surfaces basic analytics — all in one mobile-first app. Success is a shopkeeper ending the day knowing exactly what sold, what's low, and who owes them money.

## Brand Personality

Modern, confident, sharp. The app signals that this business owner is running a real operation. It doesn't feel Western or corporate — it feels built for this market, this currency, this context. Tone is direct and practical; never patronizing, never playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS (Salesforce-style): dense panels, enterprise toolbars, information overload
- Consumer fintech (Venmo, Cashapp): too playful, social-app energy, emoji-heavy
- Legacy accounting software (Tally-style): dated, spreadsheet-heavy, no visual hierarchy
- Overly Western startup (Linear/Notion): too minimal and abstract for a Nepali shopkeeper's workflow

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence through clarity** — Every number, status, and action is immediately legible. No ambiguity about state, price, or quantity.
2. **Built for the counter** — Designed for quick glances and one-handed use under real-world shop conditions. Primary actions are never more than two taps away.
3. **Sharp and decisive** — No hedging in the UI. One primary action per screen context. No three-step flows for one-step tasks.
4. **Consistent hierarchy** — Same conceptual weight gets the same visual treatment everywhere. Primary actions don't become tertiary three screens away.
5. **Local by default** — Nepalese Rupee (NPR / Rs.) throughout. Bilingual-ready layout. Familiar metaphors over imported SaaS conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum on all text
- Minimum 44×44pt touch targets on all interactive elements
- Support for `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Bilingual-ready layout (EN / नेपाली toggle in place)
- Font sizes no smaller than 11pt on mobile; body no smaller than 13pt
